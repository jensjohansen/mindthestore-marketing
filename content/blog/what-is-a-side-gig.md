A side gig is not a side hustle. A hustle is labor — trading your time for money. A gig is ownership — building something that generates income whether you're working or not.

The difference matters. If you drive for a rideshare app, that's a hustle. You stop driving, the money stops. If you own a YouTube channel with evergreen content and affiliate links, that's a gig. The videos you made last month can earn money this month.

## Why now?

Two things changed:

1. **AI can write, design, and post content.** What used to take 10-20 hours per week — writing blog posts, scripting videos, posting to social media — can now be done by AI agents in minutes.

2. **You don't need to be technical.** You bring what you know — your experience, your perspective, your interests. The AI handles the rest.
